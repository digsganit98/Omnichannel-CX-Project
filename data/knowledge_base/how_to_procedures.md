# BFSI Knowledge Base — How-To Procedures

## Cards

Q: How do I block my debit or credit card if it is lost or stolen?
A: Block your card immediately to prevent misuse. You can request a block by messaging us on WhatsApp or replying to our support email with your registered details, or by calling the 24/7 card-blocking hotline at 1800-200-1948. You can also block the card yourself from the mobile banking app under Cards, or through the net-banking portal. Once blocked, a card cannot be reused; you can then request a replacement card, which is usually delivered to your registered address within 7 to 10 working days.

Q: How do I request a replacement card after blocking?
A: After a card is blocked, request a replacement through the mobile banking app under Cards, through the net-banking portal, or by contacting support on WhatsApp or email. The replacement is issued to your registered address. For security, a new card number and CVV are generated, and you will need to set a new PIN on first use.

## Net-banking and login

Q: How do I reset my net-banking password?
A: To reset a forgotten net-banking password, open the net-banking portal and select "Forgot Password". You will be asked to verify your identity using your registered mobile number or email through a one-time password (OTP), and in some cases your card or account details. After verification, you can set a new password. For security, choose a strong password and do not reuse old passwords. If you are unable to reset it online, contact support on WhatsApp or email, or call the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947.

Q: I am locked out of net-banking after multiple failed attempts. What do I do?
A: For your security, access is temporarily locked after several failed login attempts. The lock usually clears automatically after some time. To regain access sooner, reset your password from the net-banking portal using OTP verification, or contact support on WhatsApp or email so the team can help verify your identity and restore access.

## Payees and beneficiaries

Q: How do I register a new payee or beneficiary for fund transfers?
A: Add a payee through the mobile banking app or the net-banking portal under Payees or Beneficiaries. You will need the beneficiary's account number and IFSC code (or UPI ID). After adding a beneficiary, a short cooling-off period applies before you can transfer the full limit to them, as a fraud-prevention measure. During this period, transfers to the new beneficiary may be restricted to a lower amount.

Q: Why can't I transfer the full amount to a newly added beneficiary?
A: Newly added beneficiaries are subject to a cooling-off period and a temporary lower transfer limit. This protects you against fraud if your account is ever compromised. Once the cooling-off period has passed, the standard transfer limits apply automatically. No action is needed from you during this period.

## Contact details (mobile and email)

Q: How do I update my registered mobile number or email address?
A: Keeping your contact details current is important because they are used for OTPs and alerts. You can update your registered mobile number or email through the mobile banking app or the net-banking portal under Profile. For security, changes require identity verification, and KYC validation may be needed. Some updates take effect after a short verification delay, during which alerts may be sent to both the old and new contact for confirmation.

## Statements

Q: How do I generate or download my account statement?
A: You can view and download account statements from the mobile banking app or the net-banking portal under Statements. Choose the account and the date range you need, then download the statement as a PDF. You can also request a statement for a specific period by messaging us on WhatsApp or email, and the team will share it through a secure channel after verifying your identity.

## Closing an account

Q: How do I close my account?
A: To close an account, ensure the balance is settled and any linked services, standing instructions, or auto-debits are cancelled first. Submit a closure request through the net-banking portal, or contact support on WhatsApp or email to start the process. Identity verification is required. Any remaining balance is transferred to a nominated account, and you will receive a confirmation once the closure is complete.

## Security reminders

Q: What should I never share when using these services?
A: Never share your PIN, password, full card number, CVV, or any OTP with anyone, including people claiming to be from the bank. The bank will never ask for your OTP, PIN, or password. If anyone asks for these, do not share them and report it to support immediately.
