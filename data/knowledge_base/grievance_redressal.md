# BFSI Knowledge Base — Grievance Redressal

## Raising a complaint (Level 1)

Q: How do I raise a complaint?
A: You can raise a complaint by messaging us on WhatsApp, through the mobile banking app or the net-banking portal under Complaints or Help, or by calling the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947. Please describe the issue clearly and include any relevant transaction or account details. You will receive a complaint reference number (for example, GRV-12345678) to track your case. Most complaints are resolved at this first level.

Q: How do I check the status of my complaint?
A: Use the complaint reference number you received when the case was logged. You can check the status in the mobile banking app or net-banking portal under Complaints, or by sharing the reference number with us on WhatsApp or the helpline. Our team will tell you the current stage and the expected resolution time.

## Escalating within the bank (Level 2)

Q: My complaint was not resolved. How do I escalate it within the bank?
A: If your complaint is not resolved satisfactorily at the first level, or you are not happy with the response, you can escalate it to the bank's Grievance Redressal Officer or Nodal Officer. Quote your existing complaint reference number when you escalate, so the history is available. The escalation contact details are published on the bank's official website under the grievance redressal section.

## Escalating to the regulator / Ombudsman (Level 3)

Q: What if the bank still does not resolve my complaint?
A: If the bank does not resolve your complaint within the timeframe specified under RBI rules (typically around 30 days), or if you are not satisfied with the resolution, you can approach the RBI Ombudsman under the Reserve Bank — Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS). This service is free for customers. You can file a complaint with the RBI Ombudsman online through the RBI's complaint portal.

Q: Where do I escalate insurance or investment complaints?
A: For insurance-related complaints not resolved by the insurer, you can approach the IRDAI through its grievance portal (Bima Bharosa) or the Insurance Ombudsman. For complaints relating to securities and investments, you can use SEBI's SCORES platform. In all cases, you should first give the company the opportunity to resolve the complaint before escalating to the regulator.

## General guidance

Q: What information should I keep when raising a complaint?
A: Keep your complaint reference number, the date you raised it, copies of any messages or emails, and details of the transaction or service involved. This makes escalation faster if it becomes necessary. Always note the timeline, because escalation to the Ombudsman is generally available only after the company has had the chance to resolve the issue within the specified period or has rejected it.
