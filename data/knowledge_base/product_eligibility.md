# BFSI Knowledge Base — Products and Eligibility

This covers the products offered and who is eligible. Final approval depends on documents, credit score, and verification, and is at the bank's discretion. To apply or check eligibility, use the mobile banking app, the net-banking portal, message us on WhatsApp, or call the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947.

## Loans

Q: What loans do you offer and who is eligible?
A: Available loans include:
- Smart Personal Loan: salaried or self-employed, minimum income ₹25,000/month, age 21–60.
- Dream Home Loan: age 18–70, stable income, good credit score.
- Drive Easy Car Loan: salaried or self-employed, minimum income ₹20,000/month, age 21–65.
- Future Builder Education Loan: Indian national, admission to a recognized institution, co-applicant required.
- Business Growth Loan: business vintage over 3 years, minimum annual turnover ₹10 lakhs.
- Gold Loan Express: age 18–70, gold purity 18–24 carat.
Eligibility also depends on your credit profile and documents.

Q: Which loan suits me?
A: It depends on your need: a personal loan for general expenses, a home loan to buy or build property, a car loan for a vehicle, an education loan for studies (needs a co-applicant), a business loan for an established business, or a gold loan against gold you own. Each has its own eligibility, listed above. Share your requirement on WhatsApp or call the helpline and we can guide you to the right product.

## Insurance

Q: What insurance products do you offer and who is eligible?
A: Available insurance plans include:
- Secure Life Term Plan and Smart Secure / Family Shield Term Plans (term insurance): age 18–65 (non-smoker preferred for some).
- Health Shield Plus (health insurance): age 18–65, a medical check-up may be required.
- Auto Guard Comprehensive (auto insurance): valid driving license and vehicle registration.
- Home Protector Insurance: property ownership proof.
- Travel Safe Insurance: valid passport and travel itinerary.
- Whole Life, Endowment, ULIP, and Retirement plans (e.g. Wealth Maximizer ULIP, Future Assure Endowment, Golden Years Retirement Plan): typically age 18–60, varying by plan.

Q: What is the difference between term, ULIP, endowment and whole-life plans?
A: A term plan is pure life cover at a low premium, paying your nominees if something happens to you during the term. A ULIP combines life cover with market-linked investment. An endowment plan gives life cover plus assured savings on maturity. A whole-life plan covers you for your entire life. Retirement plans help build a pension corpus. Eligibility ages vary by plan; share your need and we will suggest a suitable option.

## Deposits

Q: What deposit products do you offer?
A: Deposit products include Fixed Deposit Plus (a lump-sum deposit for a chosen tenure) and Recurring Deposit Advantage (a fixed monthly deposit). Both require valid KYC and a minimum deposit amount. They offer assured returns at a rate fixed when you book the deposit. See the interest rates knowledge for indicative rates.

## Applying

Q: How do I apply for a product?
A: You can apply through the mobile banking app or the net-banking portal under Products or Apply, or by contacting us on WhatsApp or the helpline. You will need KYC documents and, depending on the product, income proof, property or vehicle documents, or a medical check-up. After you apply, eligibility and documents are verified before approval.
