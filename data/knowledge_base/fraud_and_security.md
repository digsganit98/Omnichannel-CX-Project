# BFSI Knowledge Base — Fraud and Security

## Reporting fraud

Q: I think there is an unauthorised or fraudulent transaction on my account. What should I do?
A: Act immediately. First, block the affected card or freeze the account to stop further misuse — you can do this in the mobile banking app under Cards, through the net-banking portal, or by calling the 24/7 fraud and card-blocking hotline at 1800-200-1948. Then report the unauthorised transaction so a dispute case can be raised. Reporting as soon as you notice the issue is important, because under RBI guidelines your liability for unauthorised transactions is limited when you report promptly. Keep any reference number you receive.

Q: What happens after I report a fraudulent transaction?
A: Once reported, a dispute case is logged and our team begins an investigation. The disputed amount may be handled provisionally while the case is reviewed, depending on the type of transaction and your bank's policy. You will be kept informed of the outcome. You can check the status of your case by messaging us on WhatsApp or calling the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947.

## What the bank will never ask

Q: Will the bank ever ask for my PIN, OTP, password or CVV?
A: No. The bank will never ask you to share your PIN, password, full card number, CVV, or any OTP — not over phone, email, SMS, WhatsApp, or any link. Anyone asking for these is attempting fraud, even if they claim to be from the bank or the police. Never share them, and report the attempt to us immediately.

## Common scams

Q: What are common scams I should watch out for?
A: Be cautious of: phishing emails or SMS with links asking you to "verify" or "update" your details; vishing calls from people posing as bank staff asking for OTPs or card details; fake KYC-update messages threatening account suspension; UPI "collect requests" — remember you never need to approve a request or enter your UPI PIN to receive money; and fake reward, refund, or lottery links. When in doubt, do not click links or share details; contact the bank directly using a trusted number.

Q: How can I tell if a message or call is genuine?
A: Genuine bank communication never asks for your full credentials or OTP. Be suspicious of urgency, threats, or unexpected links. Do not call back numbers given in suspicious messages; instead use the official helpline. Check that any banking website address is correct before logging in, and only use the official mobile banking app.

## Safe-banking practices

Q: What are good habits to keep my banking secure?
A: Use a strong, unique password and change it periodically. Enable transaction alerts so you notice activity quickly. Never save passwords on shared or public devices, and always log out after using net-banking. Keep your registered mobile number and email up to date so you receive OTPs and alerts. Avoid banking over public Wi-Fi. Review your statements regularly and report anything unfamiliar.

## Disputing a card transaction (chargeback)

Q: How do I dispute a card transaction I did not authorise or that went wrong?
A: You can raise a dispute for an unauthorised card transaction, a failed transaction where money was deducted, or a charge that does not match what you expected. Report it through the app, the net-banking portal, or by contacting support, and provide the transaction details. The dispute is reviewed and, where applicable, a chargeback is initiated with the merchant or network. Resolution timelines depend on the transaction type and network rules.
