# BFSI Knowledge Base — Claim Filing Guide (Documents & Timelines)

This lists the documents usually needed to file a claim and indicative settlement timelines. Exact requirements depend on your policy. For help, use the app or portal, message us on WhatsApp, or call the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947.

## Health insurance claims

Q: What documents do I need for a health insurance claim?
A: Typically you need: the duly filled claim form, your health card or policy number, the hospital's discharge summary, itemized hospital bills and payment receipts, prescriptions and diagnostic reports, and your ID proof. For cashless claims, the network hospital handles most paperwork after pre-authorization. For reimbursement, submit the original documents to the insurer or TPA within the timeframe stated in your policy.

Q: How long does a health claim take to settle?
A: For cashless claims, pre-authorization is usually granted within a few hours to a couple of days, and final settlement happens with the hospital. For reimbursement claims, settlement typically takes about 15 to 30 days after all valid documents are received, subject to verification.

## Motor insurance claims

Q: What documents do I need for a motor (car) insurance claim?
A: Typically: the claim form, a copy of the vehicle's registration certificate (RC), your driving license, the policy document, a repair estimate, and photographs of the damage. For theft or third-party claims, a police FIR is required. For cashless repair, use a network garage. Indicative settlement is about 7 to 15 days after assessment by the surveyor.

## Life insurance claims

Q: What documents are needed for a life insurance (death) claim, and how long does it take?
A: A death claim typically requires: the claim form, the original policy document, the death certificate, the nominee's ID and bank details, and any medical or cause-of-death records if requested. As per IRDAI rules, life insurers are required to settle a claim within 30 days of receiving all required documents; if an investigation is needed, it should be completed within the regulator-specified period.

## Travel insurance claims

Q: What documents do I need for a travel insurance claim?
A: Depending on the claim, you may need: the claim form, your policy and passport copies, boarding passes or tickets, original bills (medical, baggage, or other expenses), and supporting evidence such as a flight-delay certificate from the airline or a police report for theft. Submit documents within the timeframe specified in your policy.

## General

Q: How do I start a claim?
A: Intimate the claim as early as possible through the app or portal, on WhatsApp, or via the helpline. Keep your policy number ready and follow the document checklist for your claim type. Submitting complete documents the first time helps avoid delays in settlement.
