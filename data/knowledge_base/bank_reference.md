# BFSI Knowledge Base — Bank Reference (Codes and Identifiers)

This explains the common codes used in banking and where to find them. The sample codes below are for illustration only. For your account's exact codes, check your cheque book, passbook, the mobile banking app, or net-banking, or call the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947.

## IFSC code

Q: What is an IFSC code and how is it formatted?
A: An IFSC (Indian Financial System Code) is an 11-character code that uniquely identifies a bank branch for electronic transfers such as NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS. The format is: the first 4 characters are letters identifying the bank, the 5th character is always 0 (reserved), and the last 6 characters identify the branch. For example, a code like BANK0001234 follows this pattern. You will find your branch IFSC on your cheque leaf, passbook, and in the app under account details.

## MICR code

Q: What is a MICR code?
A: MICR (Magnetic Ink Character Recognition) is a 9-digit code printed at the bottom of a cheque that is used in cheque clearing. The 9 digits represent the city code, the bank code, and the branch code. For example, a MICR like 400123456 follows this format. You can find your MICR on your cheque leaves and in your account details.

## When to use IFSC vs MICR

Q: When do I use IFSC and when do I use MICR?
A: Use the IFSC when making electronic transfers (NEFT, RTGS, IMPS) — you provide the beneficiary's account number and their branch IFSC. MICR is used in physical cheque clearing and is read automatically from the cheque. For digital transfers, IFSC is the code you need; you generally do not need to enter MICR yourself.

## Card numbers and networks

Q: How can I tell which network my card belongs to?
A: The first digit of a card number indicates the card network (the Major Industry Identifier): cards starting with 4 are Visa, cards starting with 5 are Mastercard, cards starting with 6 are RuPay or Discover, and cards starting with 3 are American Express. For example, an illustrative debit card starting 4XXXXX is a Visa card, and one starting 6XXXXX is a RuPay card. The network logo is also printed on the card. Never share your full card number.

## Finding your codes

Q: Where do I find my account number, IFSC, and other codes?
A: Your account number, IFSC, and MICR are printed on your cheque book and passbook, and are shown in the mobile banking app and net-banking under account details. For card details such as the network, refer to the card itself or the card section in the app. If you cannot find a code, contact us on WhatsApp or call the helpline and we can help after verifying your identity.
