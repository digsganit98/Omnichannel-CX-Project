# BFSI Knowledge Base — Interest Rates

All rates below are indicative and change frequently with market and RBI policy. For today's exact rate, check the mobile banking app or the net-banking portal, or call the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947. Your actual rate can depend on tenure, amount, and your credit profile.

## Savings accounts

Q: What interest do I earn on my savings account?
A: Savings account interest is indicatively around 2.75% to 3.5% per annum, often varying by your balance slab, and is usually credited quarterly. Higher balances may earn a higher rate in some account variants. Check the app or net-banking for the rate applicable to your account.

## Fixed and recurring deposits

Q: What are the interest rates on Fixed Deposits (FDs)?
A: FD rates are indicatively in the range of about 3.0% to 7.25% per annum, depending on the tenure you choose, from 7 days up to 10 years. Generally, medium tenures offer the most attractive rates. Senior citizens typically earn an additional rate of about 0.50% per annum over the general rates. Rates are fixed for the chosen tenure once the FD is booked.

Q: What are the interest rates on Recurring Deposits (RDs)?
A: RD rates are indicatively around 5.5% to 7.0% per annum, depending on the tenure. Like FDs, RDs lock in the rate at the time of booking, and senior citizens may receive a higher rate. RDs let you invest a fixed amount every month for a chosen period.

## Loans

Q: What are the interest rates on home and other loans?
A: Indicative ranges are: home loans from about 8.5% per annum, car/auto loans about 9% to 11%, education loans about 9% to 13%, personal loans about 11% to 18%, business loans about 12% to 18%, and gold loans about 9% to 15% per annum. For floating-rate retail loans, the rate is linked to an external benchmark such as the RBI repo rate (External Benchmark Lending Rate), so it moves when the benchmark changes. Your actual rate depends on the loan amount, tenure, and credit profile.

## Credit cards

Q: What interest is charged on credit cards?
A: If you do not pay the full outstanding amount by the due date, the revolving balance attracts a finance charge of indicatively about 3.0% to 3.75% per month, which is roughly 36% to 45% per annum, and the interest-free period is lost. To avoid interest, pay the total amount due in full by the due date. Paying only the minimum due keeps the account current but interest continues to accrue.

## How rates change

Q: Why did my floating loan rate or deposit rate change?
A: Floating loan rates are linked to an external benchmark such as the RBI repo rate, so they rise or fall when the benchmark is revised. Deposit rates are revised by the bank from time to time based on market conditions, but a rate locked in on an existing FD or RD does not change for its tenure. For the latest applicable rates, check the app or net-banking.
