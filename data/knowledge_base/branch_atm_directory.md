# BFSI Knowledge Base — Branch and ATM Directory

To find your nearest branch or ATM, use the branch/ATM locator in the mobile banking app or on the bank's website, which shows live details by your location. The entries below are examples. For help, message us on WhatsApp or call the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947.

## Finding a branch or ATM

Q: How do I find my nearest branch or ATM?
A: Use the branch/ATM locator in the mobile banking app or on the bank's website. Enter your city, area, or PIN code to see the nearest branches and ATMs, along with their address, working hours, and services. ATMs operate 24/7. You can also ask us on WhatsApp and we can guide you.

## Branch hours

Q: What are typical branch working hours?
A: Branches are generally open from 10:00 to 16:00 on weekdays and 10:00 to 14:00 on working Saturdays. Branches are usually closed on the 2nd and 4th Saturdays of the month, on Sundays, and on public holidays. ATMs and digital banking are available 24/7. Specific branches may vary, so check the locator for exact timings.

## IFSC codes

Q: What is an IFSC code and where do I find mine?
A: An IFSC (Indian Financial System Code) is an 11-character code that identifies a specific bank branch and is used for NEFT, RTGS, and IMPS transfers. It is printed on your cheque book and passbook, and is shown in the app and net-banking under account details. You can also find a branch's IFSC using the locator.

## Services at branches and ATMs

Q: What can I do at a branch versus an ATM?
A: At a branch you can open accounts, submit documents, complete KYC, get demand drafts, access lockers, and get in-person help. At an ATM you can withdraw cash, check your balance, get a mini-statement, change your PIN, and at many ATMs deposit cash or cheques. Most everyday tasks can also be done in the mobile banking app or net-banking without visiting a branch.
