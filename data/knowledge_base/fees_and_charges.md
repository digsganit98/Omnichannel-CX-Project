# BFSI Knowledge Base — Fees and Charges

All charges below are indicative and may vary by your account or card variant. Goods and Services Tax (GST) applies on banking service charges as per government rules. You can check the exact charges for your account in the mobile banking app or the net-banking portal, or by messaging us on WhatsApp.

## ATM withdrawals

Q: Are there charges for ATM withdrawals?
A: Withdrawals at your own bank's ATMs are free for the first 5 transactions each month, after which a charge of about ₹20 per transaction applies. At other banks' ATMs, the first 3 transactions per month are typically free in metro locations, after which around ₹21 per transaction applies. As per RBI guidelines, a minimum number of free ATM transactions is provided each month; the exact free limit depends on your account type.

## Fund transfers

Q: What are the charges for NEFT, RTGS, IMPS and UPI?
A: Online NEFT and RTGS through the app or net-banking are free. At a branch, NEFT is about ₹2.50 to ₹25 depending on the amount slab, and RTGS is about ₹25 to ₹50. IMPS is about ₹5 for transfers up to ₹1 lakh and about ₹15 above ₹1 lakh. UPI transfers, both to individuals and to merchants, are free. Charges may vary by your account variant.

## Minimum balance

Q: Is there a penalty for not maintaining the minimum balance?
A: If the required average monthly balance is not maintained, a non-maintenance charge of about ₹150 to ₹600 per month applies, depending on your account variant and location. You can see your required minimum balance and current average balance in the mobile banking app or net-banking portal. Some account types, such as basic savings accounts, do not require a minimum balance.

## Cards

Q: What are the annual fees and late-payment charges on cards?
A: Debit card annual fees are about ₹150 to ₹750 depending on the card variant. For credit cards, a late-payment fee of about ₹500 to ₹1,300 applies based on the outstanding amount slab if the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date. Paying at least the minimum amount due on time helps you avoid late fees. GST applies on these charges.

## Cheque return

Q: What is the charge if my cheque bounces?
A: If a cheque is returned unpaid due to insufficient funds or a technical reason, a cheque return charge of about ₹300 to ₹500 per instance applies. Repeated returns may also affect your banking relationship. To avoid this, ensure sufficient balance before a cheque is presented.

## Loan foreclosure and prepayment

Q: Is there a charge for foreclosing or prepaying my loan?
A: For floating-rate retail loans taken by individuals, foreclosure and prepayment charges are generally not applicable, as per RBI guidelines. For fixed-rate loans and certain other loans, a foreclosure or prepayment charge of about 2% to 4% of the outstanding amount may apply. You can check the exact terms in your loan agreement or by contacting support.

## Statements and alerts

Q: Are there charges for statements and SMS alerts?
A: Viewing and downloading statements in the app or net-banking is free. A duplicate or physical statement may cost about ₹50 to ₹100. SMS alert charges are about ₹15 to ₹25 per quarter. Email alerts are free.

## Disputing a charge

Q: I was charged a fee I do not understand. What do I do?
A: If you see a charge you did not expect, first check the charge details in the app or net-banking, as many charges relate to ATM usage, minimum balance, or card fees. If it still appears incorrect, contact us on WhatsApp, or call the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947, and our team will review the charge and explain or reverse it if it was applied in error.
