# BFSI Knowledge Base — Network Providers (Cashless Hospitals & Garages)

To find a network hospital or cashless garage near you, use the network locator in the mobile banking/insurance app or on the insurer's website, which lists current providers by location. The examples below are illustrative. For help, message us on WhatsApp or call the 24/7 helpline at 1800-200-1947.

## Cashless vs reimbursement

Q: What is the difference between a cashless claim and a reimbursement claim?
A: In a cashless claim, you receive treatment at a network hospital and the insurer settles the bill directly with the hospital through the Third Party Administrator (TPA), so you pay little or nothing upfront (except non-covered items). In a reimbursement claim, you pay the hospital yourself, then submit the bills and documents to claim the amount back. Cashless is available only at network hospitals; reimbursement can be used at any hospital.

## Finding a network provider

Q: How do I find a network hospital or cashless garage?
A: Use the network/provider locator in the app or on the insurer's website and search by city, area, or PIN code to see network hospitals (for health insurance) or cashless garages (for motor insurance). The locator shows the current list, which changes over time, so always check it before planned treatment or repair. You can also ask us on WhatsApp.

## Pre-authorization

Q: Do I need approval before a cashless hospital admission?
A: For planned hospitalization, request cashless pre-authorization through the network hospital's insurance desk a few days in advance. For emergencies, intimate the insurer or TPA within 24 to 48 hours of admission, as per your policy terms. Pre-authorization confirms coverage so the hospital can offer cashless treatment.

## TPA

Q: What is a TPA and what do they do?
A: A Third Party Administrator (TPA) is an organization that processes health insurance claims on behalf of the insurer. The TPA issues your health card, approves cashless requests at network hospitals, and coordinates claim settlement. Your policy documents and health card show your TPA's name and contact details.
